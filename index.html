EV-ADED Solar Simulator. Sun model: site latitude, declination ±23.44° by season, solar time, full azimuth geometry projected onto the wing cross-plane for any van heading. Load model: F = W·rcg·L(θ)/(a·b) per wing across 2 actuators; sinθ cancels between gravity torque and the linkage’s effective arm, so force rises monotonically with θ and peaks at the highest reachable angle. Arm suggestion solves L(15°)=retracted and L(165°)=extended exactly, using the full stroke. Reach turns red when the entered arms can’t cover 15–165° with the entered actuator lengths. Wind: q=0.00256·V² psf on full wing area, C=1.3·cos(tilt) with ±0.15 gust floor, force at the panel mid-depth centroid (slide-outs add area, not lever). Wind loads grow as cot(tilt) through the linkage, so steep dawn/dusk angles are the danger — stow flat above the shown wind limit. * = load reversal (actuator in tension, wind under the leeward wing). Gas-strut assist and van shielding excluded (conservative); stowed shows 0 (transit latches). Interlock: wings deploy before slides extend; slides retract before wings fold. Output assumes clear sky, 0.85 system derate.
